# Communication Plan
## Insider Threat Incident – University Finance Department

### 1. Purpose
Provide structured communication steps for notifications, reporting, and updates during and after security incidents.

---

## 2. Stakeholders
- IT Security Team
- Finance Department
- University Leadership
- HR Department
- Staff affected by the incident

---

## 3. Communication Objectives
- Ensure staff are informed of security changes
- Promote quick reporting of suspicious activity
- Prevent misinformation
- Maintain transparency during the investigation

---

## 4. Communication Steps

### 4.1 Immediate Notifications
- IT alerts finance leadership of suspicious access
- Staff notified if their accounts are affected
- Temporary account locks communicated via secure email

### 4.2 Reporting Requirements
- Staff must report suspicious login alerts immediately
- IT provides a hotline + ticketing system for incident reports
- Logs of after-hours access must be recorded and reviewed

### 4.3 Ongoing Updates
- Weekly updates during investigation
- Monthly cybersecurity bulletins after restoration
- Training reminders every semester

---

## 5. Training & Awareness
- Mandatory phishing and security training every semester
- Quick-reference “credential safety” guide emailed to staff
- Posters reminding staff not to leave passwords on desks

---

## 6. Communication Channels
- Secure email notifications
- University ticketing system
- Scheduled briefings with management
- Emergency alerts for critical incidents

---

## 7. Summary
Clear and consistent communication ensures staff awareness, reduces risks, and strengthens the university’s ability to detect and respond to threats quickly.
