# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) & Access Control Policy

## 1. Purpose
To protect the university’s sensitive financial and payroll systems through enforced MFA and strict access control rules.

## 2. Scope
Covers:
- All Finance Department staff
- Payroll system users
- Administrative accounts

---

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 Password Standards
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
- No reused passwords
- No personal information allowed (birthdays, names)

### 3.2 Multi-Factor Authentication
- MFA is mandatory for all payroll and finance systems
- Acceptable MFA methods:
  - Duo
  - Microsoft Authenticator
  - Google Authenticator
  - Hardware tokens (if issued)
- Remote access **requires** MFA at all times

### 3.3 Least Privilege
- Access granted only based on job duty
- Privileges reviewed quarterly
- Immediate access removal upon role change or termination

### 3.4 Credential Hygiene
- No passwords stored on sticky notes or visible locations
- Use approved password manager
- Lock workstations when unattended

---

## 4. Enforcement
- Users violating MFA rules may have accounts disabled
- Repeated noncompliance escalated to HR
- System logs monitored continuously for authentication failures

---

## 5. Review Cycle
This policy is reviewed **every 12 months** or after any major security incident.
